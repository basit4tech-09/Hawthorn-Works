Who We Are

More Than Just an Office

Hawthorn Works was founded on a simple belief — that every professional deserves a workspace that works as hard as they do. We source, inspect, and upgrade used office spaces with cutting-edge home automation technology.

Our spaces aren't just places to work. They're environments engineered for focus, efficiency, and control. From smart lighting that adjusts to your workflow to climate systems that respond to your presence — every detail is intentional.
